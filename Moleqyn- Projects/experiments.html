💧 Acid-Base Titration (Volumetric Analysis)
Class 11-12 Quantitative
Determine the exact concentration of an unknown acid or base by carefully adding a standard solution until the indicator changes colour at the equivalence point.

Materials Required

  • Burette & Pipette
  • Conical Flask
  • Standard NaOH / HCl
  • Phenolphthalein Indicator

Key Objectives

  • Measure volumes accurately
  • Observe indicator colour change
  • Calculate molarity (M₁V₁ = M₂V₂)

🔋 Construction of Galvanic Cells
Class 12 Electrochemistry
Construct a Daniell cell (Zn-Cu) to measure standard electrode potentials. Explore how concentration and different electrodes affect the total cell voltage (EMF).

Materials Required

  • Zn and Cu electrodes
  • ZnSO₄ and CuSO₄ solutions
  • Salt Bridge (KNO₃)
  • Voltmeter & Wires

Key Objectives

  • Identify Anode and Cathode
  • Understand electron flow direction
  • Apply the Nernst Equation

🎈 Verification of Gas Laws
Class 11 Physical Chem
Investigate the relationship between Pressure, Volume, and Temperature of an ideal gas. Test Boyle's, Charles', and Gay-Lussac's laws experimentally.

Materials Required

  • Sealed Gas Syringe
  • Pressure Sensor
  • Temperature Control System

Key Objectives

  • Plot P vs V curves (Boyle's Law)
  • Observe V vs T directly (Charles')
  • Understand PV = nRT

⚛️ Rutherford's Gold Foil Experiment
Class 9, 11 Atomic Structure
Replicate the historical 1911 experiment that discovered the atomic nucleus. Fire alpha particles at a thin gold foil and observe the scattering pattern on a fluorescent screen.

Materials Required

  • Alpha Particle Source
  • Ultra-thin Gold Foil
  • ZnS Fluorescent Screen

Key Objectives

  • Observe pass-through (empty space)
  • Observe deflections (positive nucleus)
  • Disprove Plum-Pudding model

💠 Molecular Geometry & VSEPR
Class 11 Chemical Bonding
Predict and build 3D molecular shapes based on Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion theory. See how lone pairs compress bond angles in water and ammonia.

Materials Required

  • 3D Molecule Builder Engine
  • Protractor (Virtual)
  • Electron Pair visualiser

Key Objectives

  • Distinguish Electron vs Molecular Geometry
  • Calculate steric number & hybridisation
  • Understand polarity (dipole moments)
