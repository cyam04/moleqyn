Why Moleqyn Virtual Laboratory?

Reinventing science education by removing physical constraints and hazards.

🏫

Traditional School Lab

  • ⚠️
    Safety Hazards: Risk of chemical spills, acid burns, and broken glassware during titrations.
  • ⏳
    Time & Access Constraints: Limited to 45-minute practical sessions once a week.
  • 👁️
    Subatomic reactions invisible: Students see color changes but cannot observe electrons, bonds, or orbitals.
  • 📉
    No Undo or Re-runs: Single experimental mistake wastes chemicals and requires full restart.
⚗️

Moleqyn Interactive Ecosystem

  • ✅
    100% Zero-Risk Practical Learning: Run complex exothermic reactions and toxic tests with total safety.
  • ✅
    24/7 Unlimited Self-Paced Experiments: Practice titrations, gas laws, and equations as many times as needed.
  • ✅
    Subatomic Visualization: Watch electrons jump energy levels, bonds form, and orbitals spin in 3D.
  • ✅
    Instant Thermodynamic Feedback: Live pH meters, temperature sliders, pressure gauges, and colorimetric indicators.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Moleqyn for study, boards, and school teaching.

Is Moleqyn aligned with the latest NCERT and CBSE syllabus? +
Yes, 100%! Moleqyn covers pure Chemistry curriculum from Class 6 up to Class 12, including the new "Curiosity" framework for middle school and rationalized CBSE/ICSE board syllabus. All chapters link directly to interactive simulations and syllabus notes.
Can I use Moleqyn on mobile devices and tablets? +
Moleqyn is designed with responsive HTML5 Canvas technology and works smoothly on desktop, tablets, smart boards, and mobile browsers without requiring any plugin or heavy installation.
What makes AtomVerse different from standard periodic tables? +
AtomVerse models all 118 elements with live revolving Bohr orbital shells, interactive 3D s/p/d/f orbitals, quantum number breakdowns (n, l, m, s), isotopic abundance charts, Hund/Pauli electronic spin practice, and emission spectral lines.
Is Moleqyn completely free for students and schools? +
Yes. Moleqyn is built to provide high-quality interactive science education to every student without financial barriers or subscription paywalls.
