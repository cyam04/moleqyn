🚀 Middle School Chemistry Missions
Class 6 • Curiosity Ch 9
🧊
Matter & Separation Detective

Discover how particles vibrate in solids, slip in liquids, and zip in gases. Test separation techniques: filtration, evaporation, winnowing and magnetic sorting.

XP: +150 XP
Class 7 • Curiosity Ch 5
🍋
Kitchen Acid & Base Explorer

Natural indicators in everyday life! Use turmeric and red cabbage extract to test whether your lemon juice, soap water, and baking soda are acidic, basic, or neutral.

XP: +200 XP
Class 7 • Physical & Chemical
🌋
Physical vs Chemical Shifts

Melting wax vs burning wick! Rusting iron vs cutting paper! Discover evidence of chemical reactions: bubbles, color change, heat, and new substances.

XP: +180 XP
Class 8 • Atoms & Elements
⚛️
The Elementary Atom Builder

Build the building blocks of the universe! Assemble protons in the center, snap neutrons into place, and watch electrons orbit in shells to make Hydrogen, Carbon, and Oxygen.

XP: +250 XP
Class 8 • Combustion & Flame
🕯️
Candle Flame Architecture

Look inside a burning candle flame. Explore the dark inner zone, luminous middle zone, and the non-luminous hottest outer zone used by goldsmiths.

XP: +220 XP
Class 8 • Metals & Non-metals
🔔
Metals vs Non-Metals Test Bench

Hit with a hammer (malleability), ring the bell (sonority), and complete the electrical circuit. Watch sulfur snap while copper stretches into fine wire!

XP: +210 XP
