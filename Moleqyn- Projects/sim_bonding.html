📖 VSEPR & Bonding Theory

Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion Electron pairs repel each other in 3D space: LP–LP > LP–BP > BP–BP. Lone pairs exert greater repulsion because they are held by only one atomic nucleus, causing compression of bonded angles!

🔗 Orbital & Resonance Analysis
