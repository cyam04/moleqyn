Excitation Theory
Thermal Excitation: Heat from the Bunsen burner excites valence electrons of metal cations into higher energy quantum shells (e.g. 3d → 4p).
Photon Emission: When electrons drop back to ground state, they release energy according to Planck's equation:
ΔE = h·c / λ Each cation emits a characteristic visible spectral color.
