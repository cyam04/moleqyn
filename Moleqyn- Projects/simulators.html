Chemistry Simulators

Select a module below to launch the interactive particle engine. Designed specifically to visualize NCERT and CBSE concepts for Class 6 to 12.

⚛️
Class 9 - 12 & Competitive

AtomVerse: Atomic Structure & Quantum Lab

Interactive 118-element Bohr model, revolving electron orbits, 3D s/p/d/f orbitals, quantum numbers, emission spectra & Hund/Pauli practice.

⚗️
Universal Sandbox • Class 10 to 12

Universal Chemical Reaction Lab & AI Predictor

Input ANY custom chemical reaction or reactants from your mind! Predict products, precipitate colors, gas effervescence (H₂, CO₂), virtual beaker mixing, and exact algebraic stoichiometric balancing.

❄️
Class 9 - 11

States of Matter & Universal Particles

Control temperature and test custom substances or elements (Water, Oxygen, Iron, Ethanol, Mercury, or ANY custom compound) with real thermodynamic phase transitions.

💧
Class 10 - 12

Acid-Base Titration

Perform precise virtual titrations. Monitor pH changes, use indicators, and find equivalence points safely.

⚛️
Class 11

Chemical Bonding

Explore VSEPR theory. Build 3D molecules, understand bond angles, polarity, and molecular geometry.

🎯
Class 9 & 11

Rutherford's Model

Recreate the famous Gold Foil experiment. Fire alpha particles and observe atomic nuclei deflection.

🎈
Class 11

Ideal Gas Laws

Experiment with Boyle's, Charles', and Gay-Lussac's laws. Alter volume, pressure, and temperature interactively.

🔋
Class 12

Electrochemistry

Construct Galvanic and Electrolytic cells. Choose electrodes, salt bridges, and measure cell potentials.

🔮
Class 11 & 12

3D Molecule & Hybridization Viewer

Interactive 3D ball-and-stick models for Water, Methane, Benzene, Ethanol. Inspect bond angles, sp/sp²/sp³ hybridization & net dipole vectors.

🔄
Class 11 & Competitive

Chemical Equilibrium & Le Chatelier Lab

Haber process (N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃). Test real-time temperature, pressure, concentration shifts, and catalyst dynamics on Kc/Qc.

🔥
Class 9 to 12 Practicals

Flame Test & Qualitative Salt Analysis

Perform Bunsen burner excitation tests for Cu²⁺, Na⁺, Sr²⁺, K⁺, Ba²⁺, Li⁺. Observe characteristic visible spectral wavelengths and Planck photon theory.

🌡️
Class 11 Physical Chem

Calorimetry & Enthalpy of Reaction

Insulated coffee-cup calorimeter. Test exothermic neutralization (HCl + NaOH) and endothermic cold-pack salts (NH₄NO₃) with live q = mcΔT thermodynamics.

☢️
Class 12 & Competitive

Radioactive Decay & Half-Life Simulator

Track 400 probabilistic parent-daughter nuclei for Carbon-14, Uranium-238, and Iodine-131. Real-time first-order kinetics N(t) = N₀e^(-λt).

🏷️
Class 10 to 12 & Competitive

IUPAC Nomenclature Engine

Interactive 2D carbon backbone builder. Apply longest chain, lowest locant rule, functional group priority order, and Class 12 coordination complexes naming.
