💧 Acid-Base Titration (Volumetric Analysis)

Class 11 - 12 NCERT Practical Quantitative
Determine the exact molarity and strength of an unknown acid/base solution by standardized volumetric titration using phenolphthalein and methyl orange indicators.

🧪 Materials & Reagents

  • 50 mL Glass Burette & 10 mL Pipette
  • Standard 0.1 M Oxalic Acid / NaOH
  • Conical Flask & White Tile
  • Phenolphthalein & Methyl Orange

🎯 Key Objectives & Theory

  • Identify concordant readings (±0.05 mL)
  • Observe sharp color transition at equivalence
  • Calculate unknown strength via molar stoichiometry
M₁V₁ / n₁ = M₂V₂ / n₂ Launch Virtual Lab ▶

🔋 Construction of Galvanic Cells & EMF

Class 12 CBSE Board Exam Electrochemistry
Construct a Daniell cell (Zn-Cu) to measure standard electromotive force (EMF) and explore the influence of ion concentrations on cell potential using the Nernst equation.

🧪 Materials & Reagents

  • Zinc & Copper Strips (Electrodes)
  • 1.0 M ZnSO₄ & 1.0 M CuSO₄ Solutions
  • Agar-Agar KCl / KNO₃ Salt Bridge
  • High-impedance Digital Voltmeter & Leads

🎯 Key Objectives & Theory

  • Identify anode oxidation (Zn) and cathode reduction (Cu)
  • Prevent liquid junction potential via salt bridge
  • Verify standard potential E°cell = 1.10 V
E_cell = E° - (0.0591/n) log Q Launch Virtual Lab ▶

🔥 Flame Test & Qualitative Cation Analysis

Class 11 - 12 NCERT Practical Qualitative
Identify alkali and alkaline earth cations (Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, Sr²⁺, Ba²⁺, Cu²⁺) via thermal electronic excitation, characteristic emission wavelengths, and blue glass inspection.

🧪 Materials & Reagents

  • Platinum / Nichrome Loop Wire
  • Concentrated Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)
  • Non-luminous Bunsen Burner Flame
  • Cobalt Blue Glass Filter

🎯 Key Objectives & Theory

  • Convert salts into volatile chlorides
  • Observe brick-red (Ca²⁺), crimson (Sr²⁺), apple-green (Ba²⁺)
  • Connect flame emission to Planck equation ΔE = hc/λ
ΔE = h·c / λ Launch Virtual Lab ▶

🌡️ Enthalpy of Neutralization (Calorimetry)

Class 11 NCERT Practical Thermodynamics
Determine the enthalpy of neutralization for strong acid-strong base (HCl + NaOH) and dissolution enthalpy of ammonium nitrate using an insulated coffee-cup calorimeter.

🧪 Materials & Reagents

  • Insulated Polystyrene Cup & Lid
  • Beckmann Precision Digital Thermometer (0.01°C)
  • 1.0 M Standardized HCl & NaOH Solutions
  • Magnetic Stirrer & Water Equivalent Mass

🎯 Key Objectives & Theory

  • Measure maximum temperature increase ΔT
  • Calculate heat released q = (m_sol + W) · c · ΔT
  • Confirm standard ΔH_neut ≈ -57.3 kJ/mol for strong pairs
q = m · c · ΔT | ΔH = -q / n Launch Virtual Lab ▶

🎈 Verification of Ideal Gas Laws (Boyle & Charles)

Class 11 Physical Chem
Investigate the macroscopic relationship between Pressure, Volume, Temperature, and Molar Mass for ideal gases. Generate real-time isothermal and isobaric curves.

🧪 Materials & Reagents

  • Hermetically Sealed Variable-Volume Gas Syringe
  • High-Precision Digital Manometer (kPa)
  • Thermo-regulated Water Bath (-50°C to +300°C)
  • Pure He, N₂, and CO₂ Test Gases

🎯 Key Objectives & Theory

  • Plot P vs 1/V linear relationship (Boyle's Law)
  • Extrapolate V vs T curve to absolute zero (Charles' Law)
  • Determine universal gas constant R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)
P · V = n · R · T Launch Virtual Lab ▶

🎯 Rutherford's Gold Foil Alpha Scattering

🧪 Materials & Reagents

  • Radioactive Polonium/Radium Alpha Source

  • Rotatable Zinc Sulfide (ZnS) Scintillation Screen
  • Vacuum Chamber & Particle Deflection Counter

🎯 Key Objectives & Theory

  • Notice >99% alpha particles pass straight through undeflected
  • Detect rare 1-in-8000 deflections at angles >90°
  • Disprove Thomson's Plum Pudding model
