Law of Conservation of Mass (Antoine Lavoisier, 1789) Matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. The total number of atoms of each element on the reactant side must exactly equal the total number of atoms on the product side. Balancing is accomplished solely by adjusting stoichiometric coefficients, never by altering chemical subscripts!
