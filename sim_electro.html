📖 NCERT / Board Master Concept

Galvanic Cell (Daniel Cell) Left half-cell: Zn oxidizes (Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻). Electrons travel via external wire to right half-cell. Cu²⁺ ions receive electrons and reduce onto cathode (Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu). The inverted U-tube salt bridge with agar-agar + KCl allows K⁺ and Cl⁻ migration, preserving electroneutrality.
