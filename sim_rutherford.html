📖 Landmark Chemistry Discovery

Rutherford's 1911 Landmark Conclusion 1. Over 99% of α-particles pass straight through undeflected → Atom is mostly empty space.
2. A small fraction deflect at significant angles → Positive charge is concentrated in a tiny central core (the Nucleus).
3. Only about 1 in 20,000 backscatters (>90°) → "It was as if you fired a 15-inch artillery shell at a piece of tissue paper and it came back and hit you."
